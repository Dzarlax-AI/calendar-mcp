Personal calendar connection and synchronization

Calendar platform

The purpose of Calendar platform is to let one person connect, view, manage, and synchronize calendars across Google Calendar, Microsoft 365, and Apple Calendar from one private web application and MCP server. Its operator can connect multiple provider accounts, manage calendar events, and create explicit one-way synchronization rules between calendars.

GOOGLE CALENDAR ACCESS

Why Calendar platform requests Google Calendar data

When its operator connects a Google account, Calendar platform uses Google Calendar access to list available calendars; read event details, dates, time zones, and recurrence; and create, update, move, or delete events only when the operator requests an action or enables a synchronization rule. OAuth credentials are stored encrypted. Google Calendar data is not sold or used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for complete access, storage, sharing, retention, and deletion details.
